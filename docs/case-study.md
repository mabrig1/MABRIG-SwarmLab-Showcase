# Engineering case study

## Problem
Multi-agent workflows can be difficult to explain, inspect and govern.

## Approach
SwarmLab explores the concept through a browser-based scripted simulation with mission selection, specialist visualization, progress and report exports. Its documentation describes human-reviewed handoffs to related education and development products.

## Engineering discussion
Separate simulated status from verified execution; distinguish completion from evidence; require human approval before external effects; make workflow state inspectable.

## Evidence boundaries
The documented lab is illustrative, not a live autonomous AI system. No claim is made about model inference, production deployment, accuracy or benchmark performance. The public sample is independently authored for this portfolio and is not the original implementation.
